Two projects that show how I work: one built by hand, one built on a fast-moving operations team.

Project 1: Sustainable Architecture & Solar Integration Model

Prince George's County Summer Youth Employment Program

Summary: Developed during the Prince George's County Summer Youth Employment Program. Researched future trends in sustainable architecture and constructed a physical model house featuring fully integrated solar panels, supported by 5 weeks of rigorous virtual technical sessions and project deliverables.

What I built

  • Painted the house like a real home with a realistic roof and a porch light, and hid all the wiring inside the roof with a small battery box for the porch light.
  • Wired the roof-mounted solar panel to two lights, which was difficult and took a long time but was worth it.
  • Sealed the door with weather strips and covered the windows with transparent paper taped on all four sides.
  • Insulated the walls with paper towels and a soft material, and lined the roof with aluminum foil.

Final test
